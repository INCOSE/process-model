This view provides information on the Measurement process in the systems engineering life cycle. By clicking on the process, you can review its purpose, while clicking on its inputs and outputs will show you their definitions. You can navigate back to the overview by clicking on the rectangular labeled "01 - System Life-Cycle Processes".
