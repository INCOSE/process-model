Knowledge Management ()
As stated in ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288, The purpose of the Knowledge Management process is to create the capability and assets that enable the organization to exploit opportunities to re‐apply existing knowledge. See detailed description in the INCOSE Handbook v.4, page 158
