System analysis report ()
An account prepared for interested parties in order to communicate the status, results, and outcomes of the system analysis activities. Includes the results of costs analysis, risks analysis, effectiveness analysis, and other critical characteristics analysis. Also includes all models or simulations that are developed for the analysis
