Measurement report ()
An account prepared for interested parties in order to communicate the status, results, and outcomes of the measurement activities. Includes documentation of the measurement activity results, the measurement data that was collected and analyzed and results that were communicated, and any improvements or corrective actions driven by the measures with their supporting data
