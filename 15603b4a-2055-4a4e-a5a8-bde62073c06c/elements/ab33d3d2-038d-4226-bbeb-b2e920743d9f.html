Concept of operations (ConOps) ()
The ConOps is a verbal and/or graphic statement prepared for the organization’s leadership that describes the assumptions or intent regarding the overall operation or series of operations of the enterprise, to include any new capability (ANSI/AIAA, 2012; ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148, 2011)
