Quality Management ()
As stated in ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288, [6.2.5.1] The purpose of the Quality Management process is to assure that products, services and implementations of the quality management process meet organizational and project quality objectives and achieve customer satisfaction. See detailed description in the INCOSE Handbook v.4, page 156
