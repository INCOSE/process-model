Preliminary life cycle concepts ()
Preliminary articulation of the various life cycle concepts consistent with the business needs in the form of life cycle concept documents on which the system of interest is based, assessed, and selected. The architecture is based on these concepts, and they are essential in providing context for proper interpretation of the system requirements. Typical concepts include: Acquisition concept, Deployment concept, OpsCon, Support concept, Retirement concept
