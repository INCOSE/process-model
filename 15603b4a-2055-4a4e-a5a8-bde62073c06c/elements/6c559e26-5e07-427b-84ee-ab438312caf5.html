Transition report ()
An account prepared for interested parties in order to communicate the status, results, and outcomes of the transition activities. Includes documentation of the transition results and a record of any recommended corrective actions, such as limitations, concessions, and ongoing issues. Should also include plans to rectify any problems that arise during transition
