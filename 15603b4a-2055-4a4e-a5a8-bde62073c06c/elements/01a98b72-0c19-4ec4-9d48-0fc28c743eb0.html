Stakeholder needs and requirements definition strategy ()
Approaches, schedules, resources, and specific considerations required to reflect consensus among the stakeholder classes to establish a common set of acceptable requirements. Includes the approach to capture the stakeholder needs, transform them into stakeholder requirements, and manage them through the life cycle
